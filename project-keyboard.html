Custom Mechanical Keyboards
← Back to all projects
June 2022Started
12+Keyboards built
OngoingStatus

I've built over a dozen custom mechanical keyboards, researching switches, keycaps, PCBs, and cases based on price, acoustic signature, and material. Every build starts the same way — picking a switch and case combo that fits the sound and feel I'm after, then sourcing parts.

Once parts arrive, I lubricate switches, tune stabilizers, solder PCBs, and add any modifications the build calls for. I also troubleshoot circuits and flash QMK/VIA firmware to set up custom layouts and hotkeys for each board.
