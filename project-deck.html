I'm working with my dad to measure, design, and build a new deck for our backyard — my first project that starts fully in CAD before any wood gets cut.

I'm modeling, dimensioning, and calculating measurements for the deck in AutoCAD, comparing wood versus composite materials on structural integrity, durability, price, and maintenance, then adjusting the CAD drawings as we run into real physical and assembly limitations on site.

EDIT: once the build is further along, add details here — which material you ended up choosing and why, any load calculations, and what changed between the CAD model and the finished deck.
